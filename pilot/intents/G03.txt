Tabs with unsaved-changes guard: switching tabs with edits prompts "discard?" Two tabs named "Profile" and "Notes" (tab semantics: a tab list with tabs and tab panels). Each tab has one multi-line text field. Typing in the text field of the current tab makes that tab "dirty". Switching to the other tab while the current tab is dirty asks "Discard changes?" with a "Discard" button and a "Cancel" button (either a browser confirm() dialog or an in-page dialog). Cancel stays on the current tab and keeps the typed text; Discard switches tabs and drops the text. Switching while the tab is not dirty switches immediately with no prompt.
