To-do app: add, complete, delete, filter All/Active/Done, count of active items The app has a text field labelled "New task" and an "Add" button. Each task shows a checkbox, its text and a "Delete" button. Three filter buttons are named "All", "Active" and "Done". A line shows the number of tasks that are not done, written "N items left" (for example "3 items left", "1 item left").
