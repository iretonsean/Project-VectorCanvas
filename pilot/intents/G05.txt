Wizard whose step 3 is skipped when the user answers "no" in step 2 A four-step wizard. Exactly one heading is visible at a time and it starts with "Step N" (N = 1 to 4), for example "Step 2: Pet". Every step has a "Next" button (on step 4 it may be named "Finish") and every step except the first has a "Back" button. Step 2 asks "Do you have a pet?" with two radio buttons named "Yes" and "No". Step 3 ("Step 3: Pet name") has a text field and is skipped when the answer to step 2 is "No". Step 4 is a summary. "Back" always returns to the step the user actually came from.
